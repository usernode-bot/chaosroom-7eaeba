# ChaosRoom

Rival trading houses, built together. A game we build as a group: run your
own house, hire workers who pay gold tribute every turn, buy tools, and
challenge the game's rival houses on market days to win their gold and
climb the rankings. Every challenge, hiring, purchase and tribute is
written into the house ledger.

- **Your house** — founded the first time you open the app, with 10,000
  gold and 50 workers.
- **Turns** — every 30 minutes each worker pays 3 gold tribute; missed
  turns are added when you come back.
- **Competition and standing** — competition (workers plus trade tools)
  is what ranks the houses; standing (half your workers, plus 40 per
  guard contract) guards your treasury on a lost market day.
- **Recruit and Tools** — hire workers (200 gold each) and buy Hand Carts,
  Iron Ploughs and Mill Wheels, which add competition. Market Clerks add
  none; they make market-intelligence reports sharper.
- **Market intelligence** — pay 300 gold for a report on a rival house:
  a rough range for its competition and gold, always true, narrower with
  more Market Clerks. One report per rival per turn.
- **Guard contracts** — 1,500 gold each; each adds 40 standing, which
  keeps part of your gold when you lose a market day, and all of it once
  standing matches the rival's competition.
- **Challenge** — tap a rival house, then challenge it to a market day.
  If your competition is greater you win a share of their gold: 10%, or
  15% with a lead of one and a half times, 20% at twice. If it is not,
  the rival can win up to 5% of your gold, kept down by your standing.
  Each rival can be met once per turn, and the market-day report shows
  the rival's exact competition after a meeting.

Single player against eight rival houses run by the game. Cross-player
rivalry comes later.

Built on [Homeroom](https://app.onhomeroom.com); see `CLAUDE.md` for the
app's own notes and the platform rules.
